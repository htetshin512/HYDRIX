About HYDRIX

HYDRIX is an educational website created by three Year 9 students to explain drought and water shortages in Myanmar. Our project brings together environmental research, useful information and practical steps people can take to prepare before water supplies become limited.

What Does HYDRIX Do?

HYDRIX helps people learn about drought, why water shortages happen and what families and communities can do to prepare.

Our website explains different types of drought, the effects of limited rainfall and the challenges water shortages can create for farming, health and everyday activities. We give particular attention to Myanmar's Central Dry Zone, including areas in Mandalay, Magway and Sagaing, where managing seasonal water supplies is important for many communities.

Alongside background information, visitors can explore water-saving methods, steps for household preparedness, frequently asked questions and interactive learning materials. We have organized these topics to make the website useful for students and general readers, not only people already familiar with environmental issues.

Our Project Goals

These two goals guide how we develop HYDRIX: the change we hope to encourage and the information our website provides to support it.

01 / VISION
What we believe

Our Vision

Our vision is to see communities across Myanmar become more informed about drought risks and the importance of protecting available water resources. We want people to understand how water shortages can affect households, farms and schools.

We hope better access to environmental information encourages responsible water use, attention to local conditions and early planning for periods when water supplies are limited.

HYDRIX cannot prevent drought, but it can help visitors make informed decisions and understand why preparedness matters.

AWARENESS / CONSERVATION / PREPAREDNESS
What we do

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a clear and accessible educational website explaining the causes and consequences of drought, with particular attention to Myanmar's Central Dry Zone. We focus on information visitors can connect to real-life challenges involving water access, farming and daily needs.

HYDRIX brings together research summaries, water conservation strategies, household preparedness guidance and interactive activities. We aim to explain these subjects in straightforward language and organize them so information is easy to find.

Through this project, we want to help visitors identify practical steps they can take to save water and prepare before shortages become serious.
